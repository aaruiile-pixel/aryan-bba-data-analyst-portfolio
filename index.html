My roadmap

From business student to analyst.

A practical sequence that grows with your BBA studies.

01 · Strengthen Excel

Practise lookup formulas, SUMIFS, IF, PivotTables, charts and data cleaning. Finish one end-to-end sales analysis.

02 · Learn SQL fundamentals

Work with SELECT, WHERE, GROUP BY, JOIN and CASE using a small business dataset.

03 · Build a Power BI dashboard

Import and clean data, define a few useful measures, then explain the key takeaways.

04 · Publish and iterate

Add project write-ups, screenshots, a resume and professional contact links. Ask for feedback and improve.
